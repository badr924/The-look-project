# E-Commerce & Retail Analytics Dashboard

An interactive 3-page analytics project covering **Sales Overview**, **Marketing & Customer Funnel**, and **Inventory Optimization with Sales Forecasting** to support data-driven retail decisions.

---

## Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Overview (Sales & Revenue Performance)
* **Key Metrics:** Tracked **125,226 total orders**, **£10,827,119 in total sales**, **£5,618,764 in profit**, and **£5,208,355 in total cost**[cite: 8].
* **Geographic Breakdown:** **China**, the **United States**, and **Brazil** lead revenue performance, followed by **South Korea** and **France**[cite: 8].
* **Category & Trend Analysis:** Monitored revenue growth across years (peaking in **2023**) and highlighted top-performing categories such as **Outerwear & Coats**, **Jeans**, and **Sweaters**[cite: 8].

### 2. Marketing Dashboard
* **Customer Base & Retention:** Monitored **80,044 active customers** and **30,212 repeat customers**, reflecting a **37.7% retention rate** and **62.3% churn rate**[cite: 7].
* **Conversion Funnel by Channel:** Compared **Total Sessions**, **Cart Sessions**, and **Purchase Sessions** across **Email**, **AdWords**, **Facebook**, **Organic**, and **YouTube**[cite: 7].
* **Cart Abandonment Patterns:** Analyzed abandonment rates by marketing channel and day of the week to identify high drop-off points (such as **YouTube** and **Friday**)[cite: 7].

### 3. Inventory & Sales Forecasting
* **Stock & Sell-Through Metrics:** Tracked **490,705 total units**, **181,759 sold units**, **308,946 in-stock items**, and a **37.04% sell-through rate**[cite: 6].
* **Category & Demand Comparison:** Evaluated profit margins versus return rates by category alongside a 91-day comparison of stock levels versus items sold[cite: 6].
* **Forecasting:** Included a forward-looking **Sales Forecast** projecting **2,257,666** total units (upper bound: **2,467,718**, lower bound: **2,047,613**)[cite: 6].
